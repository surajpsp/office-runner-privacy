Privacy Policy

Office Runner · Effective date: 28 September 2026

In short: Office Runner is an offline game. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it does not send your personal data to us or to anyone else. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Office Runner (package ID com.silentdev.officerunner) is developed by Silent Dev ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the game handles information when you play it on Android or iOS.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no servers, user accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics tools or crash-reporting services of its own, and it does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

3. Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the game keeps the following data locally on your device only:

  • Optional names you type in, such as employee, manager and company names (nicknames are fine, and every name is optional)
  • A randomly generated in-game employee ID used for fun (for example, "#EMP-2026-123")
  • Game progress: coins, scores, best distance, runs, XP, outfits, upgrades, mission stars and daily-reward streak
  • Settings such as sound on/off and whether the tutorial has been completed

This data never leaves your device through the game, and we cannot see it.

4. Sharing your results

If you choose to share a result card, the game shows you a preview first and then opens your device's standard share menu. The shared image hides your employee, manager and company names. Nothing is shared unless you tap share yourself. Once you send the image to another app, that app's privacy policy applies.

5. Device backups

Your phone's operating system (for example, Android Backup or iCloud) may include the game's saved data in your device backups. Those backups are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies, and you can manage them in your device's backup settings.

6. App stores

When you download the game, Google Play or the Apple App Store may collect information such as download statistics, or crash reports if you have allowed them. That collection is done by those platforms under their own privacy policies, not by the game:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

7. Deleting your data

  • Remove names only: open Profile → Privacy & Info → Clear Personal Names. Your coins and progress stay.
  • Remove everything: uninstall the game, or clear the app's storage in your device settings.

8. Children's privacy

Office Runner is a family-friendly cartoon game. Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, we do not knowingly collect children's data. Parents with questions can contact us at the email below.

9. Security

Your data stays in the game's private storage on your device, so it is never sent over the internet by us. Please keep your device secure (for example, with a screen lock) to protect your saved data.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that handle data differently (for example, online leaderboards or ads), we will update this page and change the effective date above before those features are released.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, email us at surajpsp@example.com.
